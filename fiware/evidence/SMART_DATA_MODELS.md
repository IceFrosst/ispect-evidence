# Smart Data Models: what fits, what we use, why

Checked 8 Nov 2026 against `github.com/smart-data-models` (e.g. `dataModel.Alert`, read from `alert-schema.json`).

## Candidate checked: `Alert` (domain Alert)
`Alert` = "an alert generated by a user or device in a given location". Properties: `dateIssued`, `validFrom`, `validTo`,
`severity`, `category` (enum: traffic, naturalDisaster, weather, environment, health, security, agriculture), `alertSource`, `data`
(+ `subCategory`, `location`, `address` from the commons).

**Why we did not retype our entities as `Alert`:**
- Our `Finding` is a *screening result* (a candidate for expert review, "not a finding of actual interference"). Calling it an
  `Alert` with a `severity` would present it as an operational warning, which is exactly what the product says it is not.
- The `category` enum has no radio-spectrum or interference value; forcing `environment` or `security` would be wrong data.
- Our core attributes (`outcome`, `margin_db`, `reason_code`, `limitCitation`, `affectedStations`) have no counterpart in `Alert`.
  We found no Smart Data Model for ITU filings or radio-coordination screening.

So the vocabulary is a **project context** (`infra/fiware/context.jsonld`, `@vocab https://sinceai.example.org/fiware/`), and the repo says so
in `evidence/vm/current/note.md`. We do not claim Smart Data Model conformance.

## SDM and NGSI-LD conventions we do follow
- Every attribute is an NGSI-LD `Property`, `Relationship` or `GeoProperty` with `observedAt` (the pipeline's `generated_utc`).
- Position is a `location` **GeoProperty** (GeoJSON Point) on `FinnishStation`, queryable with `georel=near;maxDistance=...`.
- Links are **Relationships** (`Finding.notice`, `.release`, `.affectedStations`, `.nearestStations`; `Notice.release`) so that the
  broker answers the graph question "which stations does this filing affect".
- Entity ids are URNs `urn:ngsi-ld:<Type>:<kind>:<ific>:<key>` (`publish.py: uid`), types are PascalCase, attributes camelCase
  (a few snake_case names such as `margin_db` carry the unit and are kept to match the result-file contract).
- `name` and `location` map to the ETSI NGSI-LD core terms in `context.jsonld`.

## If someone wants conformance later (not done)
`Alert` is the closest fit for the *notification* a user receives. A conforming projection would be a separate derived entity
(`type: Alert`, `category` omitted or a documented extension, `dateIssued` = release date, `alertSource` = the Finding URN) next to,
not instead of, the Finding. We left it out because it adds a second source of truth for little meaning.
